Why I’m Learning Cybersecurity

Why cybersecurity? Because I’ve seen the dark side of the internet—and I’ve had enough of it.

I’ve seen people hide behind the mask of “dark humor” and call themselves “sigma” while they cyberbully others. But that bullying affects real people. Last year, I came across a post about a teenage boy who loved makeup and fashion. When I looked at the comments, they were filled with hate—calling him gay, LGBTQ slurs, and other disgusting things. That boy ended up taking his own life because of it. 

And it’s not just that. I’ve seen so many cases where women are recorded without their consent—some don’t even realize it’s happening. These videos get shared or sold online. As a girl, even using social media can make me feel unsafe.

So I chose cybersecurity—not just to build a career, but to protect people. To protect data. To make sure people feel safer online—especially women and even men who go through things silently. This field gives me a purpose. And I’m just getting started.
